# Documento de levantamento e requisitos (ABNT)

## Problem

The PEI IV professor evaluates the project's requirements as an ABNT document: diagrams and
requirements are presented on 20–21/10/2026 and the documentation is posted on the AVA on
30/10/2026 (Manual do PEI, Apêndice F). Each late delivery costs 0.5 point of the final grade,
cumulatively, with no second call (§6.4).

Today the requirements of One exist only as an English Markdown spec of business rules
(`specs/mvp-business-rules/requirements.md`, 44 `BR-` rules and 9 lifecycle transitions) plus
Portuguese discovery notes. None of it is in the structure the professor grades (Apêndice D.5),
none is ABNT-formatted (§4.1–§4.2), and the artefacts that phase requires - non-functional
requirements, use cases with specifications, class diagram, conceptual and logical data models -
do not exist.

When this ships, the group has a `.docx` and a `.pdf` they can post on the AVA and present, whose
every functional requirement traces back to a rule in the spec, regenerated by one command when
the spec changes.

## Flow

Reuses `specs/mvp-business-rules/requirements.md` as the single source of the requirements and
the discovery notes as the source of the elicitation; the document is a Portuguese rendering of
them, not a second spec.

1. in (each exists): `requirements.md`, `One_MVP_Visao_Produto.md`, `One_Pesquisa_Mercado_Helpdesks.md`,
   the PEI manual -> Portuguese content written into `docs/requisitos/conteudo.md` (new, no door - placement)
2. `docs/requisitos/diagramas/*.puml` (new) -> PlantUML (door 1) renders one PNG per diagram
3. `docs/requisitos/gerar.py` (new, door 2) - reads the content, the cover metadata and the PNGs,
   writes the `.docx` with ABNT page setup, capa, folha de rosto, TOC field and numbered sections
4. Microsoft Word (exists, driven through PowerShell COM) - updates the TOC and page fields,
   saves the `.docx`, exports the `.pdf`
5. out: `docs/requisitos/One_Levantamento_e_Requisitos.docx` and `.pdf`; `test_documento.py`
   (new) reads them and `requirements.md`; `.claude/scripts/verify.ps1` (exists, door 4) is meant
   to run it - pending, the file is outside what the agent may edit

## Relations

`None - no stored-data shape change`: this feature persists nothing. The data model drawn inside
the document is content, reviewed through the criteria below.

## Surface

`None - nothing consumed outside` by a program. The document and the generator command are the
two surfaces, both walked in Observable.

## Criteria

### S1: Formatted ABNT document generated (P1)

One command produces a `.docx` and a `.pdf` that meet §4.1–§4.2 of the manual.

**Acceptance Criteria**

1. WHEN `python docs/requisitos/gerar.py` runs on a machine with Python, Java 17 and Microsoft Word THEN the generator SHALL write `docs/requisitos/One_Levantamento_e_Requisitos.docx` and `.pdf`, print both paths and exit 0
2. IF Java or Microsoft Word is not available THEN the generator SHALL exit 1, print the name of the missing tool and leave the previous `.docx` and `.pdf` unchanged
3. The document SHALL set every section to A4 with margins of 3 cm top and left and 2 cm bottom and right
4. The document SHALL set body text in Times New Roman 12 pt
5. The document SHALL set body paragraphs to 1.5 line spacing with 12 pt after each paragraph
6. The document SHALL show no page number on capa, folha de rosto, resumo and sumário, and SHALL show the page number at the top right from the first page of INTRODUÇÃO, counting the folha de rosto as page 1
7. The capa SHALL contain, centred and in this order: "UNIVAG - CENTRO UNIVERSITÁRIO", "CURSO SUPERIOR DE TECNOLOGIA EM ANÁLISE E DESENVOLVIMENTO DE SISTEMAS", the authors, the title, the subtitle, "Várzea Grande - MT" and "2026"
8. The folha de rosto SHALL contain the literal nature text of §4.1, "Trabalho apresentado à disciplina de Projeto Extensionista Integrador, do Curso Superior de Tecnologia em Análise e Desenvolvimento de Sistemas (Análise e Desenvolvimento de Sistemas) do UNIVAG, como requisito parcial para obtenção de nota semestral."
9. The resumo SHALL have at most 250 words and be followed by a line starting "Palavras-chave:" with 3 to 5 terms
10. The sumário SHALL be a Word TOC field over heading levels 1 to 3, and the `.pdf` sumário page SHALL list "INTRODUÇÃO" with a page number
11. The document SHALL number headings progressively (1, 1.1, 1.1.1) with level-1 headings in bold uppercase

**Independent test:** open the `.pdf`: cover, title page, abstract, table of contents, then page numbers starting at the introduction.

### S2: Elicitation and requirements content (P1)

The professor reads how the requirements were gathered and what they are, in the D.5 structure.

**Acceptance Criteria**

12. The document SHALL contain, in this order, the level-1 sections INTRODUÇÃO, LEVANTAMENTO DE REQUISITOS, VISÃO GERAL DO PRODUTO, REQUISITOS DO PRODUTO, DOCUMENTAÇÃO DAS FUNCIONALIDADES and CONSIDERAÇÕES FINAIS, followed by the unnumbered REFERÊNCIAS
13. The INTRODUÇÃO SHALL contain the subsections Propósito do documento, Escopo do produto, Premissas do projeto, Convenções, termos e abreviações, and Estrutura do documento
14. The VISÃO GERAL DO PRODUTO SHALL contain the subsections Descrição do produto, Usuários da solução, Abrangência e soluções similares, and Suposições e dependências
15. The DOCUMENTAÇÃO DAS FUNCIONALIDADES SHALL contain the subsections Diagrama de casos de uso, Especificações dos casos de uso, Diagrama de classes, Modelo conceitual and Modelo lógico
16. The LEVANTAMENTO DE REQUISITOS SHALL name the three techniques used - entrevista com a coordenação (two sessions), pesquisa de mercado and análise documental do Manual do PEI - and state the baseline figures with the values recorded in E1.8–E1.11 (171, 70 and 15 messages; ≈ 256; 50% and 30%; 5 h and 3 h; 10, 30 and 60 min)
17. IF a figure is recorded as unconfirmed in `requirements.md` (the unit of the 5 h / 3 h, the period of the 83 e-mails) THEN the document SHALL mark it "a confirmar" next to the figure
18. The document SHALL identify the interviewee only by role, "coordenadora dos cursos de tecnologia", and SHALL NOT contain her name
19. The functional requirements table SHALL give each row an id `RF-NN`, a description in Portuguese and an `Origem` cell citing the `BR-` or `F` ids it derives from
20. IF any of the 44 `BR-` rules or 9 `F` transitions in `requirements.md` is cited in no `Origem` cell THEN `test_documento.py` SHALL fail and name the missing id
21. The non-functional requirements table SHALL give each row an id `RNF-NN`, a category, a description and a source
22. The non-functional requirements SHALL include one row stating the PEI IV stack constraint: installable PWA, SPA front-end framework and REST back-end, naming the chosen frameworks
23. The non-functional requirements SHALL include one row requiring a visible notice that student messages are processed by an external AI provider, and that attachments are never sent to it (Q13 default)
24. The Premissas do projeto SHALL list each open question Q5–Q19 used by the document with its id, the default adopted and the words "a confirmar com a coordenação"
25. The document SHALL give each use case a specification with id `UC-NN`, atores, pré-condições, fluxo principal, fluxos alternativos, pós-condições and RFs relacionados
26. IF a `UC-NN` appears in the use-case diagram without a specification, or a specification without the diagram THEN `test_documento.py` SHALL fail and name the id
27. IF an `RF-NN` is cited by no use case's RFs relacionados THEN `test_documento.py` SHALL fail and name the id
28. The CONSIDERAÇÕES FINAIS SHALL list the questions still open with the coordination and the next PEI deliverables with their dates (27–30/10, 13/11, 20/11, 27/11)

**Independent test:** run `test_documento.py`; it reads the `.docx` and `requirements.md` and reports any untraced rule, use case or RF.

### S3: Figures, tables and references (P1)

Every illustration and table follows ABNT and is rebuilt from a versioned source.

**Acceptance Criteria**

29. The document SHALL embed four figures - casos de uso, classes, modelo conceitual and modelo lógico - each with a caption above in the form "Figura N – <título>" and the line "Fonte: elaborado pelos autores (2026)." below
30. The document SHALL give each table a caption above in the form "Quadro N – <título>" and a line starting "Fonte:" below
31. IF an embedded figure has no matching `.puml` source in `docs/requisitos/diagramas/` THEN `test_documento.py` SHALL fail and name the figure
32. IF an entity of the conceptual model has no table of the same name in the logical model THEN `test_documento.py` SHALL fail and name the entity
33. IF an author-date citation `(AUTOR, ano)` in the text has no entry in REFERÊNCIAS, or an entry is never cited THEN `test_documento.py` SHALL fail and name it
34. The REFERÊNCIAS SHALL be in alphabetical order

**Independent test:** open each figure in the `.pdf` and compare it with its `.puml`; run the test.
